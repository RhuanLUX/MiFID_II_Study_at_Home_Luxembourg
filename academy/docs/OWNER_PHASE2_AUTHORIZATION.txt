OBJECTIVE

Implement a simple knowledge-reinforcement section directly inside the existing learning platform.

This phase is NOT an examination simulator.

The objective is to help students remember and consolidate what they have just learned.

Preserve the existing application, educational content, layout, navigation, translations, notes, completion tracking and backup functionality.

1. PLACEMENT

At the bottom of EACH lesson, immediately after the existing "Personal Notes" section, add a new section:

"Knowledge Reinforcement"

The existing Previous, Mark as Completed and Next navigation controls should remain below this new section.

Keep the interface clean and visually consistent with the current application.

Do not redesign the platform.

2. NUMBER OF EXERCISES

Create EXACTLY 5 original exercises for EACH of the 95 lessons.

Total: 475 exercises.

Every exercise must be directly related to the educational content of its corresponding lesson.

Do not generate generic questions unrelated to the lesson.

Do not ask about information that has not been taught.

3. DIFFICULTY DISTRIBUTION

Each lesson must contain:

EXERCISE 1: EASY
Basic definition, terminology or concept recognition.

EXERCISE 2: EASY
Simple conceptual understanding.

EXERCISE 3: INTERMEDIATE
Understanding relationships between concepts or identifying correct financial reasoning.

EXERCISE 4: INTERMEDIATE
Applying concepts to a straightforward financial or regulatory situation.

EXERCISE 5: CHALLENGING
A more demanding question involving interpretation, calculation, a practical decision or a realistic financial scenario.

The challenging exercise must be more demanding than the others but remain fully solvable using the lesson content.

Do not introduce external knowledge requirements.

4. ALLOWED EXERCISE FORMATS

Use ONLY simple selection-based interactions.

A. MULTIPLE CHOICE

Present one question with four alternatives (A, B, C, D).

Exactly one answer must be correct.

At least two incorrect alternatives should be reasonably plausible and conceptually close to the correct answer.

Avoid absurd distractors that make the answer obvious.

B. FILL IN THE BLANK

Example:

"Compound interest means interest calculated on ______."

Provide four selectable alternatives.

The student must SELECT the correct answer, never type it.

C. MATCHING

Present three or four concepts on the left and their corresponding definitions or explanations on the right.

The student selects the correct associations.

Use simple dropdowns or click-based matching.

No dragging is required.

D. PRACTICAL SCENARIO

Present a short and realistic financial situation.

Ask the student to select the most appropriate answer among four alternatives.

For example, choosing the correct interpretation of a financial calculation, investment risk or regulatory requirement.

No written response.

IMPORTANT:

Never require typing, free-text answers, essays, uploads or external applications.

Use the exercise formats naturally.

There is no requirement to use every format in every lesson.

Avoid artificial repetition and ensure variety throughout the curriculum.

5. FEEDBACK

After the student selects an answer and clicks "Check Answer":

If correct:
- Display "Correct".
- Highlight the correct answer.
- Provide a concise explanation of why it is correct.

If incorrect:
- Display "Incorrect".
- Clearly identify the correct answer.
- Provide a complete educational explanation.

The explanation must teach the underlying concept.

Explain WHY the correct answer is correct.

For intermediate and challenging questions, also clarify why the most plausible incorrect alternatives are not appropriate.

For matching exercises, explain the correct associations.

Explanations must be meaningful and educational.

Do not use generic feedback such as:
"Because this is the correct definition."

A student who answers incorrectly should be able to understand the mistake without needing to search for another explanation.

6. LANGUAGE

English remains the primary language.

Provide corresponding Brazilian Portuguese translations for questions, alternatives and explanations, accessible through the existing eye icon translation mechanism.

Preserve financial terminology and Luxembourg regulatory meaning.

Do not change the approved English or Portuguese lesson content.

Each exercise should display:

Exercises are OPTIONAL.

Do not change the existing lesson completion rules.

No grades, performance dashboards, achievements, leaderboards or gamification.
